Who is each streaming platform, really?

They don't compete the same way. Netflix is the only one that's both popular and well rated, Max bets on quality over reach, and Peacock trails on both.

the setup

01
5platforms: Netflix, Hulu, Max, Peacock, Prime Video
2,000titles from the TMDB API, 400 per platform, half movies and half TV
1snapshot in time. Catalogs change constantly.

Streaming services keep their real numbers private, so we built our own dataset: a Python script pulled availability, genres, popularity and ratings from TMDB. Disney+ was dropped because it had too few usable titles to compare fairly. We built the analysis and dashboards in Tableau.
