Do happier players mean more money?

Not really. Across thousands of Steam games, how many people reviewed a game told us far more about its publisher's revenue than how much they liked it.

the setup

01
4data sources: Steam store, SteamSpy, IGDB and Gamalytic publisher revenue
~11,400games matched to a publisher
3signals tested: review score, review volume, player engagement

We merged the four sources in Python and SQLite, cleaned out unstable review labels and non-game entries, then compared simple regression models to see which signal best explains publisher revenue on games the models hadn't seen.

what this can't tell us

04
  • Revenue is measured per publisher, not per game, so a weak game from a giant publisher still looks "rich."
  • These are associations, not causes. Marketing spend, wishlists and store promotion weren't in the data.
  • Engagement is a snapshot (concurrent players vs. estimated owners), not long-term retention.
